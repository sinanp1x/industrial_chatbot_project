AUTOMATED TEXTILE MILL - MOTOR & MACHINERY INVENTORY
Location: Sector 4, Spinning and Weaving Facility
Document ID: INV-TEX-2026-01

================================================================================
1. SPINNING SECTION MACHINERY
================================================================================

[EQ-SPN-101] Ring Spinning Frame
- Primary Motor: 75 kW AC Induction Motor (4-pole, 1450 RPM, IE4 Efficiency)
- Drive System: Inverter-fed Variable Frequency Drive (VFD) for soft start and speed optimization.
- Spindle Speed Range: 12,000 to 18,000 RPM
- Lubrication Schedule: Automatic oil bath lubrication for main gearbox; high-speed bearing grease every 2,000 operational hours.
- Safety Interlocks: Emergency stop pull-wire, spindle guard limit switch.

[EQ-SPN-102] Heavy-Duty Carding Machine
- Primary Motor: 30 kW Induction Motor with Star-Delta Starter
- Auxiliary Motors: 3x 2.2 kW Servo Motors for feed roller and doffer synchronization.
- Drive System: Toothed timing belt drive with mechanical overload clutch.
- Lubrication Schedule: Weekly manual greasing of cylinder bearings using lithium-complex grease.
- Safety Interlocks: Card cover magnetic safety lock (prevents opening until cylinder comes to a complete standstill).

================================================================================
2. WEAVING & LOOM SECTION MACHINERY
================================================================================

[EQ-WEV-201] High-Speed Air-Jet Loom
- Primary Motor: 18.5 kW High-Torque Brake Motor
- Drive System: Direct gear drive coupled with electromagnetic clutch-brake assembly.
- Operating Parameters: Up to 850 insertions per minute; high thermal load profile due to rapid start-stop cycles.
- Cooling System: Integrated forced-air cooling fan with dust filter.
- Maintenance Note: Inspect brake disc gap bi-weekly; check compressor air-pressure sensor interlocks for the insertion nozzles.

3. FINISHING & COMPRESSOR UTILITIES
================================================================================

[EQ-UTL-301] Central Screw Air Compressor (Utility Support)
- Primary Motor: 110 kW Heavy-Duty Synchronous Motor
- Drive System: Direct drive via flexible coupling.
- Operating Pressure: 7.5 bar continuous load.
- Maintenance Schedule: Oil filter replacement every 1,000 hours; vibration analysis on motor bearings monthly.
