ABB IRB6700 Press Line - Maintenance & Troubleshooting Manual

## 1. Specifications
- Model: IRB6700 Hydraulic Press
- Normal Operating Pressure: 150 - 165 bar
- Maximum Relief Pressure: 180 bar
- Hydraulic Fluid: HLP46

## 2. Safety Guidelines (LOTO)
DANGER: Before performing any maintenance on the hydraulic relief valves or fluid lines, you MUST perform Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) on the main electrical panel (Breaker B-12).
WARNING: Ensure system pressure is completely bled off using the manual bleed valve (V-90) before loosening any fittings. High-pressure hydraulic fluid can cause severe injury.

## 3. Troubleshooting Guide

### 3.1 Hydraulic Overpressure (Fault Code: ERR_HYD_OVERPRESS)
Symptom: System pressure exceeds 180 bar and the emergency relief valve is tripped.
Root Cause: This is typically caused by an obstruction in the main return manifold or a stuck proportional relief valve.
Corrective Action Procedure:
1. Immediately execute emergency stop and initiate LOTO on Breaker B-12.
2. Verify system pressure using the mechanical gauge on the accumulator.
3. Open the manual bleed valve (V-90) to relieve remaining pressure.
4. Inspect the return manifold for debris or obstruction.
5. If the manifold is clear, replace the proportional relief valve cartridge (Part # RV-400).
6. Reset the safety PLC via the main HMI panel before restarting the press.

### 3.2 Motor Overheat (Fault Code: ERR_MOTOR_OVERHEAT)
Symptom: Main drive motor casing temperature exceeds 85°C.
Root Cause: Insufficient cooling airflow or clogged cooling fins.
Corrective Action Procedure:
1. Power down the system.
2. Inspect the rear cooling fan unit and clean out any accumulated dust from the motor fins.
3. Allow the motor to cool below 50°C before attempting restart.
